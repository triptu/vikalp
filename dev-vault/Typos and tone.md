# Notes on a slow morning

Their are three things I want to remember from this week. First, the walk to the station is shorter then it looks. Second, its fine to leave a draft unfinished overnight.

The third thing is harder to say. I keep starting essays with a sentence that tries to do to much, and then the rest of the paragraph has to clean up after it. A good opening sentence dont need help.

I want to write plainer. Not simpler, just plainer. The kind of sentence you could read aloud to a friend without them noticing it was written down. Paradigmatically, this necessitates a recalibration of my lexical predilections.
