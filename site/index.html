Vikalp

Settle on the right way to say something. Keep several versions of a word, sentence or paragraph in place. Fade text instead of deleting it. Keep spare writing nearby. Lint on demand. Ask AI for options, marks and cuts, never for prose.

Vikalp is Hindi for "alternative". An Obsidian plugin for desktop. Free and open source.

Install

In Obsidian, open Settings → Community plugins → Browse and search for Vikalp. You can also open it from the plugin directory or with the link obsidian://show-plugin?id=vikalp.

To install by hand, download main.js, manifest.json and styles.css from the latest release into <your vault>/.obsidian/plugins/vikalp/ and turn on Vikalp in Settings → Community plugins.

Then, in Settings → Vikalp, pick an AI provider for the AI features (everything else works without one) and turn on Use suggested hotkeys for ⌘J, ⌘⇧J and ⌘.. Vikalp takes no keys until you do. You can also set your own in Settings → Hotkeys.

What it does

Alternatives

Put the cursor on a word and press ⌘J. Type a version and press Return. Add as many as you like. Press ⌘J again for the sentence, again for the paragraph.

Hover the word and press the arrow keys to read each version in the note. There is no accept step. Whatever is showing is the note, and ⌘Z undoes any swap.

AI versions

Press ⌘⇧J and options stream in, marked with a small robot. Your showing version never changes. You pick.

Ghosting

Select text, press ⌘. then G. It fades but stays in the note and out of the word count. Right-click → Revive brings it back. Copy clean text copies the note without it.

Overflow

Select text, press ⌘. then S. It moves to a side panel kept with the note. Drag it back when it fits.

Linter

Press ⌘. then L. Rules for hedging, wordiness, AI-sounding phrases, LinkedIn voice, repetition, passive voice and more. It runs only when you ask. Edit the rules or add your own in settings.

The Lab

Click Lab in the status bar. Mark passes point at typos, weak or convoluted sentences, off-tone words and hedges. Trim levels strike through the words a model would cut, from 10% to 50%. You keep or cut each one. Nothing is deleted unless you choose to.

Read-through

Press ⌘. then R to hide every marker and read like a reader. Esc returns.

The guide covers every feature.

Keys

These are the suggested hotkeys. Turn them on in Settings → Vikalp → Hotkeys.

⌘JVersions for the word or selection. Again: sentence. Again: paragraph.
⌘⇧JAsk AI for versions
⌥↑ ⌥↓Switch versions from the cursor
⌘.Every other action, one letter away

Every action is also a command. Change any key in Settings → Hotkeys.

AI and privacy

  • AI never writes into your note. It can add labelled versions, mark text, or suggest cutting your own words.
  • Nothing runs without a click. There is no background AI, no telemetry and no account.
  • When you run an AI action, the text it needs goes to the provider you picked in settings: OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI with your own key, or a model on your own computer through Ollama or LM Studio. Any other OpenAI-compatible server works too.
  • You pay the provider directly. The Lab shows what each call cost. With Ollama or LM Studio the text stays on your machine and costs nothing.
  • Vikalp's data for a note stays in your vault, in .obsidian/plugins/vikalp/notes/.
