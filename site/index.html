Try it

Each step below does one thing to this note. You can click anything in it.

  1. Versions, in place

    Keep another version of a word, a sentence or a paragraph. Swap between them to read each one in place.

    try: click an underlined word

    In Obsidian: J ·

  2. Fade, don’t delete

    Fade a sentence instead of deleting it. It stays readable, leaves the word count, and comes back with a click.

    try: click the faded line

    In Obsidian: . then G ·

  3. Keep what you cut

    Move a line to Overflow, a side panel kept with the note. Put it back when it fits.

    try: click the to put it back

    In Obsidian: . then S ·

  4. Ideas when you’re stuck

    Ask AI for a few other ways to say a word or a sentence. They sit next to your own versions, marked with a robot, and you decide what fits. Your note only changes when you pick one.

    try: pick a robot version

    In Obsidian: J ·

  5. Lint when you ask

    Check your note for hedging, wordy phrases, passive voice and AI-sounding lines. Edit the rules or write your own.

    try: click a wavy line

    In Obsidian: . then L ·

  6. The Lab

    Ask a model to mark typos and weak sentences, or to strike what it would cut. You keep or cut each one.

    try: click a struck phrase

    In Obsidian: . then T ·

  7. Read it clean

    Hide every marker and read your note the way a reader will.

    try: switch it off

    In Obsidian: . then R ·

Get Vikalp

Install in Obsidian
  1. 1.In Obsidian, open Settings, then Community plugins, and search for Vikalp.
  2. 2.Put the cursor on a word and press J.
  3. 3.For AI features, add a key for OpenRouter, Anthropic or OpenAI, or run a model locally with Ollama or LM Studio. Everything else works offline.
